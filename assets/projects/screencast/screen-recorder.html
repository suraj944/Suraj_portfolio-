Tips

  • Shortcuts work while this tab is focused (browsers don't allow global hotkeys for web pages). You can always stop from the browser's “Stop sharing” bar.
  • Full HD needs a Full HD source. Recordings can't exceed the size of the screen or window you share — a 1366×768 laptop screen records at 1366×768.
  • System audio: tick “Share audio” in the picker. Tab audio works in Chrome/Edge everywhere; whole-screen audio depends on your OS and browser version.
  • Camera “Embed in video” draws your webcam into the recording itself (Chrome/Edge). “Floating bubble” pops the camera into an always-on-top window, which only shows up when you record the entire screen.
  • macOS: allow your browser under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then restart the browser.
  • MP4 (H.264) plays everywhere. WebM files get proper duration metadata automatically so they're seekable.
  • Long recordings are held in memory — download them as soon as you're done.
